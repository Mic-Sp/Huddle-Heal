Emergency & Crisis Notice: Huddle and Heal Counseling and Consulting, LLC provides scheduled outpatient psychotherapy and does not provide 24/7 emergency psychiatric services. Immediately call or text 988 to reach the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (24/7, free, confidential), call 911, or go to your nearest emergency room.
