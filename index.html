TELETHERAPY IN TN, IN & IL • FOUNDED BY EMILY SHELTON, LPC-MHSP

Step Inward from the Cold. Healing Doesn’t Happen in Isolation.

When life delivers its heaviest seasons—unresolved trauma, deep heartbreak, profound grief, or the exhausting cycle of addiction—it can feel like standing entirely alone against a bitter, unrelenting storm. You spend so much energy just trying to survive the freeze, bracing against distress, and carrying the weight on your own.

In nature, penguins survive the harshest sub-zero winds not by toughing it out alone, but by stepping inward to form a huddle. Together, they share warmth, break the force of the wind, and protect each other until steady ground returns. Founded by licensed psychotherapist Emily Shelton, Huddle and Heal was built on that exact principle. Therapy here is your collaborative sanctuary: a down-to-earth teletherapy space where you can finally set down your armor, regulate your nervous system, and gently find your footing at your own pace.

THERAPEUTIC PHILOSOPHY

You Are More Than What You Are Enduring

“You are more than what you’re enduring. All your pain is just a hero forming.”

— Citizen Soldier

Hardships and pain have a way of lingering, sometimes surfacing immediately, and other times manifesting years later in ways we don't fully expect. When distress arises, we naturally do whatever we can to survive and make sense of the changes around us. At Huddle and Heal, therapy is a grounded sanctuary to understand your past, live with clarity in the present, and prepare for the future without judgment.

Authentic & Nonjudgmental

Come As You Are

There is no need to put on a brave face or perform. Emily provides an empathetic, confidential listening ear where you are welcomed to show up as your most authentic, unfiltered self.

Collaborative Empowerment

You Steer the Ship

In our work together, you are always in the driver’s seat. Emily acts as a steady navigator, offering clinical guidance, evidence-based tools, and safety protocols, but you hold the helm and dictate the pace.

Evidence-Based Modalities

Root-Cause Healing

Rather than offering temporary band-aids, we combine psychoeducation, Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Motivational Interviewing (MI), and EMDR to dismantle negative core beliefs and help your nervous system release stored distress.
