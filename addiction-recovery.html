Addiction & Substance Use Recovery

Compassionate, evidence-based support to break free from cycles of dependence and reclaim your autonomy.

Addiction rarely begins as a quest for self-destruction; more often, it begins as an understandable attempt to soothe unbearable emotional pain, numb past trauma, or cope with a chronically overwhelmed nervous system. Emily provides a grounded, judgment-free space to help you understand your relationship with substances, regain control, and build a fulfilling life in alignment with your values.

Beyond Willpower: Understanding the Cycle of Dependence

Substance use challenges are not moral failures or signs of weak character. Repeated substance use physically reshapes the brain’s dopamine reward pathways and stress-response systems, turning what once felt like a choice into an automatic physiological survival loop. Emily meets you exactly where you are, working collaboratively to examine:

  • Alcohol & Substance Dependence: Identifying patterns of increasing tolerance, physiological cravings, loss of control, and failed attempts to moderate or stop.
  • Emotional & Habitual Triggers: Uncovering the specific environments, relationship dynamics, emotional states, and stressors that prompt the urge to use.
  • High-Functioning Substance Use: Supporting adults whose careers, parenting, or outward lives appear intact, but who internally feel trapped, exhausted, and burdened by dependency.
  • Behavioral & Process Habits: Addressing compulsive coping patterns—such as emotional spending, workaholism, or avoidance behaviors—that operate on similar neurological pathways.

Treating the Root: Dual Diagnosis & Trauma-Informed Care

Addiction rarely exists in a vacuum. Often, substances are used as an understandable survival strategy to self-medicate unresolved trauma, complex PTSD, deep grief, or chronic anxiety. If therapy only targets substance cessation while leaving underlying pain untouched, the urge to numb will persistently resurface. Emily specializes in concurrent dual-diagnosis care that treats the root causes alongside the behavioral symptoms.

  • Concurrent Dual-Diagnosis Treatment: Simultaneously addressing co-occurring mental health conditions—such as depression, anxiety, and PTSD—so your emotional recovery supports your sobriety.
  • Trauma Integration with EMDR: Utilizing Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing (EMDR) protocols to desensitize traumatic memories and somatic triggers without requiring prolonged exposure to distressing details.
  • Nervous System Stabilization: Developing somatic grounding and emotional containment techniques so you can tolerate difficult sensations without needing to turn to substances for relief.

Pathways to Sustainable Recovery

Recovery is deeply personal and never one-size-fits-all. Whether your goal is complete abstinence, intentional harm reduction, or exploring your ambivalence around alcohol or drugs, Emily partners with you in a supportive, collaborative alliance. Treatment integrates proven clinical modalities to help you cultivate lasting equilibrium:

Motivational Interviewing (MI)

Resolving internal ambivalence, uncovering your personal values, and building authentic, self-directed motivation for meaningful change.

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)

Identifying cognitive distortions, unhooking from rationalizations, and breaking automatic craving-reaction thought loops.

Proactive Relapse Prevention Planning

Mapping out subtle early warning signs, managing interpersonal boundaries, creating behavioral safety nets, and building reliable support networks.

Harm-Reduction Philosophy

Meeting you where you are, honoring incremental progress, and prioritizing your physical and emotional well-being without moral judgment or dogma.

Support for Families & Loved Ones

Addiction is rarely an isolated struggle; it reverberates throughout entire relationships and family systems, often leaving partners, parents, and loved ones exhausted by chronic crisis management, anxiety, and heartbreak. Emily provides specialized support and clinical consultation to help family members establish healthy boundaries, untangle from codependency, and reclaim their emotional well-being.

  • Boundary Setting & Detachment with Love: Learning to step out of enabling patterns and caretaking loops while maintaining compassion for your loved one.
  • Effective Communication: Shifting away from blame and reactive arguments toward non-judgmental, clear communication that encourages accountability.
  • Restoring Personal Equilibrium: Creating emotional safety, addressing secondary trauma, and tending to your own nervous system regardless of where your loved one is on their recovery path.
