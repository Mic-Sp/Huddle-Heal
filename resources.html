Somatic Grounding Tools

Two quick, self-paced nervous system regulation exercises designed to help you interrupt acute distress, re-center your senses, and signal safety to an activated sympathetic nervous system.

The 5-4-3-2-1 Sensory Grounding Technique

Orient to your current physical environment and pull focus away from internal distress by engaging each of your primary senses step-by-step:

  • 5 See: Acknowledge 5 distinct objects you can see around you right now.
  • 4 Feel: Notice 4 things you can physically feel (e.g., your feet on the floor, texture of clothing).
  • 3 Hear: Listen for 3 distinct sounds in your space (e.g., a clock ticking, distant traffic, your breath).
  • 2 Smell: Identify 2 scents around you, or recall two comforting, familiar aromas.
  • 1 Affirm: Say 1 positive or anchoring affirmation to yourself (e.g., “I am safe in this moment”).

Box Breathing (4×4)

A structured, rhythmic breathing pattern that stimulates the vagus nerve, slows your heart rate, and signals safety to an activated nervous system:

  • 1 Inhale (4 Seconds): Breathe in slowly and deeply through your nose, expanding your lower abdomen.
  • 2 Hold (4 Seconds): Gently hold the breath at the top without straining or clenching your throat.
  • 3 Exhale (4 Seconds): Release the air smoothly and evenly through your mouth like a gentle sigh.
  • 4 Pause (4 Seconds): Rest at the bottom of the breath for a count of four before beginning the next cycle.
