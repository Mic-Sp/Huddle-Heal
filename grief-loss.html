Grief & Bereavement Counseling

Honoring your loss, untangling painful emotions, and finding steady ground at your own pace.

Grief is not a clinical disorder to be cured, nor is it a rigid checklist of five neat stages. It is the natural, profound response of the mind and body to losing someone or something vital to your world. As a Certified Advanced Grief Counseling Specialist (CAGCS), Emily provides a warm, grounded sanctuary where you do not have to put on a brave face, rush your timeline, or carry the weight of heartache alone.

The Many Faces of Grief & Loss

Loss shapes our lives in diverse, deeply personal ways. Emily works collaboratively with adults navigating:

  • Bereavement & Death of a Loved One: Processing the profound absence of a partner, parent, child, sibling, or close friend—whether sudden, traumatic, or following a prolonged illness.
  • Anticipatory Grief: The quiet, exhausting weight experienced while caring for someone with a degenerative condition or terminal diagnosis.
  • Ambiguous & Non-Death Loss: Grieving significant life changes that don't involve death, including divorce, chronic illness, estranged family bonds, infertility, or career loss.
  • Pet Loss & Disenfranchised Grief: Honoring significant losses that society often minimizes, invalidates, or expects you to get over quickly.
  • Complex & Traumatic Bereavement: When grief is compounded by trauma, sudden tragedy, guilt, or unresolved conflict that complicates natural processing.

A Companioning Approach: Walking Alongside Your Grief

You cannot “fix” grief because grief is not broken. Rather than treating bereavement as an illness to cure or an obstacle to get past, Emily practices a collaborative companioning framework. We create a spacious, patient container where all feelings—anger, sorrow, numbness, relief, guilt, and confusion—are welcomed and validated without judgment.

The Companioning Philosophy

Walking alongside you into the wilderness of loss rather than directing where you should go, respecting your unique intuitive timeline.

Meaning Reconstruction & Narrative Therapy

Giving voice to unspoken stories, working through difficult “what-ifs,” and exploring how your bond with what was lost transforms over time.

Somatic Resourcing & ACT

Cultivating self-compassion and gentle nervous system regulation techniques to keep you grounded when grief waves feel overwhelming.

EMDR for Traumatic Loss

Utilizing targeted EMDR protocols when loss is sudden, violent, or complicated by intrusive distressing memories and medical trauma.

Integration, Not “Moving On”

Society often pressures grieving people to find “closure” or quickly return to business as usual. In grief counseling, the goal is never to forget, sever emotional attachments, or force yourself to “move on.” True healing is about integration—learning how to weave the reality of loss into the ongoing fabric of your life so you can carry their memory with warmth rather than sharp agony.

  • Dismantling the Myth of Closure: Releasing societal expectations and arbitrary timelines; understanding that grief comes in nonlinear waves.
  • Honoring Continuing Bonds: Developing meaningful rituals, personal traditions, and creative ways to maintain an enduring internal connection.
  • Restoring Equilibrium: Helping your nervous system gently expand its capacity to hold moments of peace, laughter, and purpose alongside your sorrow.

You Don’t Have to Carry This Alone

Grief can feel profoundly isolating, especially when the outside world expects you to keep moving forward as if nothing changed. Emily provides a steady, gentle teletherapy space where your grief is honored, never rushed or judged.
