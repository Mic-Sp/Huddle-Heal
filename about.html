About Emily Shelton

LPC-MHSP (TN) • LMHC (IN) • LCPC (IL) • CADC • CAGCS • EMDR CIT

“True healing never happens in isolation. It begins the moment you have a warm, grounded space where you no longer have to carry the weight alone.”

Warmth, Grounding, and Collaborative Care

Life transitions, trauma, and loss can leave us feeling untethered, emotionally exhausted, and disconnected. Inspired by the natural resilience of the penguin huddle, where individuals step inward from the harsh Antarctic winds to find shared warmth and co-regulation, Emily founded Huddle and Heal to provide an authentic, judgment-free sanctuary for adults ready to reclaim their lives.

Emily’s therapeutic approach is grounded, direct, and deeply collaborative. In therapy, you are always the author of your own story and in the driver’s seat of your journey. Emily acts as a steady navigator, providing evidence-based clinical roadmaps, gentle pacing, and practical tools to help you process past experiences, quiet internal chaos, and build sustainable momentum.

She integrates evidence-based modalities tailored to your unique nervous system and history, including:

Trauma Reprocessing

Eye Movement Desensitization & Reprocessing (EMDR)

To safely reprocess distressing memories and release trauma stored in the body.

Cognitive Reframing

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)

To examine and reframe unhelpful thought patterns and core beliefs.

Recovery & Change

Motivational Interviewing & Acceptance Strategies

To help you resolve ambivalence, navigate life transitions, and sustain recovery.

Clinical Consultation & Supervision

In addition to individual psychotherapy, Emily provides clinical consultation, professional supervision, and organizational advisory services grounded in trauma-informed care, nervous system regulation, and reflective practice.

EMDRIA Certification Hours

EMDR Clinical Consultation

As an EMDR Consultant in Training (CIT), Emily offers clinical consultation for EMDR-trained therapists actively working toward their EMDRIA certification. Consultation covers complex trauma case conceptualization, target sequencing, cognitive interweaves, and protocol fidelity in a supportive, growth-oriented environment.

TN • IN • IL Approved

Multi-State Licensure Supervision

Emily provides post-graduate clinical supervision for associate and pre-licensed counselors pursuing independent state licensure across Tennessee (LPC-MHSP), Indiana (LMHC), and Illinois (LCPC). Supervision emphasizes reflective clinical practice, diagnostic formulation, ethics, and vicarious trauma mitigation.
